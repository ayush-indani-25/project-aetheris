Exploring the Hidden Ocean of Europa

Project Aetheris is a daring mission to Jupiter's icy moon, Europa. Beneath its frozen surface lies a giant ocean of liquid water. Our goal is to melt through the ice, explore the dark waters below, and see if alien life is swimming in the deep.

How We Will Do It

1. Melting Through the Ice

Europa is covered in a crust of solid ice 10 miles thick. We will use a special heated drill robot to slowly melt a tunnel straight down to the water.

2. Searching for Life

Once inside the ocean, robotic submarines will explore warm underwater volcanoes. On Earth, tiny creatures live near hot ocean vents without any sunlight, so life might exist there too.

Why This Mission Matters for Our Future

Are We Alone in Space?

Finding even tiny, microscopic living things on Europa would prove that Earth is not the only place with life. It would be the biggest discovery in human history.

Building Better Spaceships

Jupiter is far away and surrounded by dangerous space radiation. Learning how to keep astronauts safe on this trip will help us build ships that can travel even deeper into space.
