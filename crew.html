Meet the Space Crew

Exploring space takes a team with many different talents—not just rocket pilots! Click the buttons below to see how biologists, engineers, doctors, and communications experts all work together on Project Aetheris.

Commander Elena Vance

Job: Mission Captain & Pilot

Elena steers the spaceship through deep space and guides the crew safely down to Europa's icy surface.

Captain David Rojas

Job: Deep-Space Navigator

David maps out the safest flight path past asteroids and calculates exact engine burns using star positions.

Lieutenant Jax Sterling

Job: Ship Systems Engineer

Jax keeps the plasma engines running, repairs ship hardware, and makes sure the crew has clean air and water.

Maya Patel

Job: Robotics & Submarine Engineer

Maya builds and controls the heated ice-drill robots and underwater drones that explore Europa's dark ocean.

Dr. Marcus Chen

Job: Space Biologist

Marcus studies ocean water samples under powerful microscopes to search for tiny living organisms.

Dr. Amara Okafor

Job: Planetary Geologist

Amara tests the thickness of Europa's frozen crust and studies underwater volcanoes on the ocean floor.

Dr. Sarah Lin

Job: Communications Expert

Sarah uses high-speed space lasers to send videos, data, and discoveries millions of miles back to Earth.

Dr. Leo Novak

Job: Space Flight Surgeon

Leo keeps the astronauts healthy, treats injuries, and protects the crew from zero-gravity muscle loss and radiation.
